# Daneshill House

**Summary**: Daneshill House is a 2-storeyed H-plan house built in 1903 by Edwin Lutyens, featuring severe Arts and Crafts style on the north and east, and Edwardian Baroque on south and west.

H-plan house of 2 storeys, with red tile roofing, walling of thin red bricks laid to Flemish bond, tile heads and cills to smaller openings. The plain north front has gabled wings (double at the east side), 2:3:1:3:1 windows, a gabled 2-storeyed porch. Most of the windows are diminutive coupled casements (with brick mullions) but there are 4, 5 and 7 unit lights in the gables. The boarded door is within an open porch formed by an arch within a moulded rectangular framework.
