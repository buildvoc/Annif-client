- Willmer House is a Grade I listed Georgian townhouse located at 38 West Street in Farnham, Surrey, England, renowned for its exceptional early 18th-century architecture and multifaceted historical roles. Constructed in 1718 by local hop merchant and maltster John Thorne as a grand display of wealth, the building features a fine red brick facade with elaborate gauged brickwork, rusticated Doric pilasters, and a bracketed cornice, contrasting with simpler construction at the rear. Architectural historian Nathaniel Lloyd praised the Doric-order brick pilasters in his 1929 work A History of English Brickwork as perhaps the most beautiful extant example.
